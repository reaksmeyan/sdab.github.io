Our position

We respect creators and copyright owners. Sdab is meant for content you own, have permission to use, or that is in the public domain or openly licensed. We ask users to confirm this for every link, and we act on valid notices from rights holders.

If you're a user

Before you download anything, make sure you have the right to. Check the licence, ask the owner, and read the source platform's terms. Copyright rules differ between countries, and this page isn't legal advice.

Reporting infringement

If you're a copyright owner (or authorised to act for one) and believe content processed through Sdab infringes your rights, send a written notice to copyright@example.com that includes:

  1. your name, address, phone number, and email;
  2. a description of the copyrighted work you say was infringed;
  3. the link that was submitted to Sdab, so we can locate the request;
  4. a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use isn't authorised by the owner, its agent, or the law;
  5. a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you're the owner or authorised to act for the owner; and
  6. your physical or electronic signature.

Please don't submit false or bad-faith notices. Knowingly misrepresenting infringement can carry legal liability.

What happens next

We review complete notices promptly. Where appropriate we'll block the reported link or source and may notify the person who submitted it.

Counter-notices

If you believe we blocked something by mistake, email the same address with the link, an explanation of why you have the right to download it, and your contact details.

Repeat infringers

We may suspend or block users who repeatedly submit content they have no right to download.
